Frequently Asked Questions & Technical Specifications

Are my uploaded documents secure?

Yes, 100%. DocuMatrix executes all operations (merging, compression, text extraction, and watermarking) locally inside your browser's WebAssembly and JavaScript environment. Your files are never transferred over the network to any third-party server.

How does the ATS Resume matcher work?

Our algorithm tokenizes your submitted resume and job description, eliminates common stop words, and evaluates keyword coverage against common recruitment software databases (e.g., Workday, Taleo, Greenhouse). It flags missing qualifications and verifies action verb density.

Is there a file size limit?

Because processing runs in your local browser, limits are dictated solely by your computer's RAM. You can comfortably merge and compress documents of 50MB–100MB without artificial restrictions.

Can I use this on mobile devices?

Yes. The entire user interface is responsive across tablets, Android devices, iPhones, and desktop browsers without requiring software installation.
